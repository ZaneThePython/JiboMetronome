# Metronome

A metronome that keeps the beat with a steady click, and Jibo dances along: his head sways side to side and he bobs on every beat. Turn the dancing off any time for a plain metronome.

## How to use it

1. Open **Metronome** from Jibo's main menu.
2. Set the tempo with the arrows above and below each digit: the left arrows change it by 100, the middle by 10 and the right by 1. Any tempo from 30 to 250 BPM works.
3. Tap **Start** (play). Jibo clicks on every beat, the light next to the tempo flashes, and he dances in time.
4. Tap the **dancing Jibo** button to switch between **Dancing** and **Still**. When it is gray he stays still and only clicks.
5. Change the tempo while it runs and the metronome picks up the new tempo a moment after your last tap.
6. Tap **Stop**, or swipe down to leave. Jibo settles back to his resting pose.

Jibo remembers your last tempo and whether he was dancing.

## Requirements

5x1 3.1.7 or newer. Works offline.
